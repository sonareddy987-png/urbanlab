One connected workflow

Clarity at every step of response.

UrbanLab brings key response activities into one prototype workspace so teams can review what was reported, what work is underway and what has been verified.

🌦️

Rainfall scenario planning

Adjust a demo rainfall level and explore how different illustrative conditions could change inspection priorities. It does not represent a live forecast.

How it works

A simple report-to-recovery loop.

Every stage has a clear next action and a visible status.

STEP 01

Report

A citizen enters a waterlogging or rain-related issue with a locality and description.

STEP 02

Review & assign

An officer reviews priority and assigns a response task to a field worker.

STEP 03

Respond

The worker records progress and submits completion notes for verification.

STEP 04

Verify recovery

The officer checks submitted work and marks the report resolved when appropriate.
